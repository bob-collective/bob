# Swap between EVM chains

Gateway also supports swaps that don't involve Bitcoin at all — moving tokens between EVM chains. Only your EVM wallet is needed.

1. **Connect your EVM wallet**

   Connect any of the 500+ supported EVM wallets.

   <video src="/user-hub/tutorial/connect-evm-wallet.mp4" controls muted loop playsInline />

2. **Set up the swap**

   Choose the asset and chain you want to receive, enter your amount, review the quote and click Swap.

   <video src="/user-hub/tutorial/evm-to-evm-setup.mp4" controls muted loop playsInline />

3. **Track your order**

   Follow along in the wallet modal at the top right, which shows progress and your Order ID. For a more detailed view, look the Order ID up in the [Gateway Explorer](https://gateway-explorer.gobob.xyz/).

   ![Swap submitted showing USDT on BNB Smart Chain and USDT0 on Arbitrum One](/user-hub/tutorial/evm-to-evm-track.png)

4. **Receive your tokens**

   Your tokens arrive at your EVM address. Most swaps between EVM chains complete within a couple of minutes, though they can be delayed by congestion on the destination network.

   ![Completed swap showing USDT sent from BNB Smart Chain and USDT0 received on Arbitrum One](/user-hub/tutorial/evm-to-evm-received.png)
