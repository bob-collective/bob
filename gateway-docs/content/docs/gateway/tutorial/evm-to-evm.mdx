---
title: Swap between EVM chains
description: Move USDT, USDC, ETH and other supported tokens across chains without touching Bitcoin.
icon: Repeat
---

Gateway also supports swaps that don't involve Bitcoin at all — moving tokens between EVM chains. Only your EVM wallet is needed.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Connect your EVM wallet

Connect any of the 500+ supported EVM wallets.

<video src="/gateway/tutorial/connect-evm-wallet.mp4" controls muted loop playsInline />

</Step>

<Step>

### Set up the swap

Choose the asset and chain you want to receive, enter your amount, review the quote and click Swap.

<video src="/gateway/tutorial/evm-to-evm-setup.mp4" controls muted loop playsInline />

</Step>

<Step>

### Track your order

Follow along in the wallet modal at the top right, which shows progress and your Order ID. For a more detailed view, look the Order ID up in the [Gateway Explorer](https://gateway-explorer.gobob.xyz/).

![Swap submitted showing USDT on BNB Smart Chain and USDT0 on Arbitrum One](/gateway/tutorial/evm-to-evm-track.png)

</Step>

<Step>

### Receive your tokens

Your tokens arrive at your EVM address. Most swaps between EVM chains complete within a couple of minutes, though they can be delayed by congestion on the destination network.

![Completed swap showing USDT sent from BNB Smart Chain and USDT0 received on Arbitrum One](/gateway/tutorial/evm-to-evm-received.png)

</Step>

</Steps>
